The kind that makes you feel better, not just look better.

After years of chronic back pain from scoliosis, I struggled to find a movement practice that felt sustainable. Running, HIIT, and online workouts often led to strain and injury, and at one point I was even wearing kneeguards on both knees to hike Bukit Timah Hill 😂

It was only when I found a yoga practice built around functional, therapeutic movement that I began to feel stronger, steadier and more at ease with my body. That practice helped ease the pain, tightness and weakness that had been holding me back 💪

Lastly, I experienced the value of practising mindfulness and moving with the breath. 🧘‍♀️

Core belief

Rushing into a pose doesn't save time. In my experience, it costs more, in injuries that take weeks or months to undo. I slow down first, and let strength and range of motion follow.

Training your mind to be patient, present, and accepting of your body's limitations is just as much a part of the practice.
